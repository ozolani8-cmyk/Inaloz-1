Problem: 256 ÷ 4

Step 1: Set up the division.
   ______
4 ) 256

Step 2: Look at the first digit of the dividend (2). 
        4 cannot go into 2, so look at the first two digits (25).
        4 goes into 25 a maximum of 6 times (4 x 6 = 24).
        Write 6 on top.

   __6___
4 ) 256
  - 24
  ______
     1

Step 3: Bring down the next digit (6) to make 16.
        4 goes into 16 exactly 4 times (4 x 4 = 16).
        Write 4 on top.

   __64__
4 ) 256
  - 24
  ______
     16
   - 16
  ______
      0

Answer: 256 ÷ 4 = 64
